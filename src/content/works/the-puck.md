A small voice assistant I'm building for my desk. When it's done, you say the wake word, ask a
question out loud and hear the answer, without leaving what you are working on.

- It is for quick questions while your hands and eyes are busy
- Your voice will stay on my home network. Only the text of the question goes to the model that answers it
- A ring of lights will show when it is listening, thinking or speaking, so you can tell what it is doing at a glance

It connects to my laptop today. The microphone and speaker come next. The puck on this desk is the
size of the real box, and its ring follows the board on the laptop.
