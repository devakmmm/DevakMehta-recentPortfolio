A model that plays Tetris by choosing where each piece should land. It looks at every possible spot
for the piece and picks the best one, so it weighs the options against each other instead of
judging each one alone.

It learned by watching a rule-based player, and in most test games it plays as well as the player
it learned from. Picking the best of several options is the shape of most real choices.
